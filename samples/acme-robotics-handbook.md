# Acme Robotics – Employee Handbook (sample document)

> Fictional company. Used to demo document Q&A with this RAG service.

## About the company

Acme Robotics was founded in 2014 in Pune, India, by Meera Kulkarni. The company builds
autonomous warehouse robots and employs about 320 people across Pune and Bengaluru.

The flagship product is the **Kestrel X2**, a warehouse robot that carries loads of up to
150 kg, runs for 10 hours on one charge and recharges fully in 90 minutes.

## Working hours

The office opens at 9:30 AM. Core collaboration hours are 11 AM to 4 PM; outside these hours
employees may plan their own time. Employees can work from home up to two days per week,
agreed with their manager.

## Leave policy

- Every employee gets **24 days of paid leave** per year.
- Unused leave of up to **10 days** carries over to the next year; anything above that lapses.
- Sick leave is separate: 12 days per year, and a doctor's note is needed for more than 3 days in a row.
- Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Expenses and travel

Submit expense claims within 30 days in the Expensely app. Domestic flights must be economy class.
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 elsewhere.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of INR 50,000 per year for courses, books and certifications.
Conference trips count toward this budget and need manager approval at least 3 weeks in advance.

## IT and security

Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Report lost
devices to it-help@acme.example within 1 hour. Never share passwords; use the company password manager.
